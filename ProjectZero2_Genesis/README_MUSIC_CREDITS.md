# Music credits

The final boss theme of Project Zero II: Genesis ("The Last Card") is **"GameChanger" boss theme by Photon Arts and Carameii**:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K_9j1k3jH-Q

The authors allow its use in non-profit projects and non-monetised or monetised YouTube videos, as long as Photon Arts and Carameii are credited as the owners of the music and their channels are linked. It may not be used in commercial projects. This game is a non-profit fan project. The file is `assets/boss_final.mp3`.

All other music in the game is synthesised in the browser by the game's own code.
