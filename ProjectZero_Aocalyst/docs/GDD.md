# Project Zero: Aocalyst, game design document

**Genre:** 2D side-scrolling action-adventure. **Platform:** any modern browser, keyboard or gamepad.

## Premise: the Book of Zero

The game is written as scripture. In the beginning there was the Void, then the First Light, the
**Aocalyst**: the stone that begins and ends all things. Five lands were given to mankind and five
seals set on the deep. The Meridian raised a tower pointing downward and dug for God, found the
Throne under Kalder, and the Throne required a lamb. They bound the nameless one, **Zero**, and
tore them in two. The seals broke, and the Day of Judgment fell. The flesh went into the ice; the
wrath went into the fire and became the **Endbringer**, the Beast.

Thirty years later Zero rises from the ash. A voice calling itself Vesper, the last in heaven, says
the Judgment is unfinished and Zero must carry the Book. Four dead rise behind Zero: War (Rook),
Death (Sable), Plague (Mirae) and Famine (Ysolde).

The five regions are the five seals: the fallen city, the mad garden, the sea of dust, the frozen
deep, the Lake of Fire. Bosses are their beasts and trumpets: ATLAS-9 the Last Trumpet, Sylvaris the
Accursed Tree of Life, Qareth the Beast from the Sea, Nadir the Beast below the Ice, Vulkaris the
Cherub of the Flaming Gate.

## Twists

1. **Vesper is dead.** The record at Nadir says the voice burned on the first day.
2. **Zero has no body.** The armour was dreamed from a corpse frozen at Nadir. The soul finds its flesh.
3. **The voice of heaven is Zero's own wrath.** The Beast raised the dead to carry the seals home.
4. **Mercy over judgment.** Zero refuses to weigh anyone. The Lamb walks to the altar by choice, the
   Book closes, and a new heaven and a new earth follow. The real Vesper answers after the credits.

## Combat rules

Attacks have startup, active and recovery frames. Enemies have poise; breaking it staggers them.
Every operator has energy (skills), cooldowns and an ultimate meter. A dodge has invulnerability
frames, and dodging in its first frames triggers a Perfect with slow motion. The Warden's early
guard frames are a Perfect Guard that staggers attackers and reflects shots.

### The Volcanic Core

- **I. The Ashen Ascent:** collapsing causeways, moving rock shelves, falling rocks, geysers, a
  hidden chamber, a pressure valve that erupts and reshapes the path, a timed lava run, an elite
  ambush.
- **II. The Forge Beneath:** staggered lifts, three valves that progressively drain the hall,
  crusher corridor, Vulkaris with periodically rising lava.
- **III. Zero Point:** descent down the Bore, Trinity door requiring all three shards, final arena
  with three phases.

The Endbringer keeps every attack from the original game. Phase 1 teaches the patterns. Phase 2
drops the floor into lava, leaving lifts, and adds the five ultimates. Phase 3 is THE TRUE VOID:
REALITY COLLAPSE, a refilled health bar, tentacles, a rebuilt stage, and the fastest patterns.

## Progression

XP and levels per operator (benched operators earn 60%), skill points, six upgrades each, ten relics
in two equipment slots, three shards with squad-wide bonuses, 19 lore logs, 9 chests, 15
achievements, autosave at beacons, three difficulties.

## Audio direction

Everything is synthesised at runtime, and every region is its own genre with its own arranger:
plainchant, organ and bells for the Book of Zero; noir jazz in Halcyon; a Celtic waltz in 3/4 in the
forest; maqam with oud, ney and darbuka in the desert; beatless glass and choir on the ice; Dies Irae
on Kalder; a 7/8 ostinato for bosses; an organ toccata over double-time Dies Irae for the Endbringer;
a chorale on the Canon progression for the ending, resolving the main theme into major. Intensity
stems fade in with combat. Plucked strings are Karplus-Strong models; choirs are formant filters.
